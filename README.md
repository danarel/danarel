## About Me

I'm a Director of Communications at a labor union in California, where I lead campaign strategy, digital organizing, press outreach, and member communications. I've spent 12+ years working across labor, nonprofit, and private-sector communications.

Outside of work, I coach youth hockey (head coach, San Diego Jr. Gulls 14U AA) and speak at national coaching conferences alongside NHL, NCAA, and junior-level coaches. I write about leadership and systems in both hockey and organizing, and I'm the author of *Parenting without God* and *The Secular Activist*, with bylines in TIME, HuffPost, Salon, and others.

## What I'm Working On

- Communications and digital strategy for my union's members and campaigns
- Coaching content and systems work for The Coaches Site and The Hockey Think Tank
- A newsletter on secular Buddhism and contemplative practice
